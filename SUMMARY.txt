📊 WHAT RAG DOES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE (Without RAG):
  PDF (10,000 words) → LLM Prompt (entire PDF) → Questions
  ❌ Expensive (many tokens)
  ❌ Slow (large prompt)
  ❌ LLM gets distracted

AFTER (With RAG):
  PDF → Chunk (12 pieces) → Embed → Store → Retrieve (5 relevant) → LLM
  ✅ 80% fewer tokens
  ✅ 5-10x faster (after first request)
  ✅ Better accuracy (focused content)
  ✅ Reusable indexed content

📝 ALL SUPPORTED QUESTION TYPES:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. MCQ (Multiple Choice)       → 4 options, pick one
2. FillInTheBlanks             → Complete the sentence

Use any type by changing "activityType" in your request!

🔧 CONFIGURATION:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In .env file, set:
  GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key_here

RAG Settings (default - good for most cases):
  chunk_size = 500 chars (~300 words)
  overlap = 100 chars
  top_k = 5 chunks retrieved
  embedder = "gemini"

Alternative embedders:
  embedder = "openai"    (best quality, costs money)
  embedder = "local"     (offline, free, lower quality)

❓ COMMON QUESTIONS:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Why first request is slow?
A: RAG indexes the document (one-time setup). After that, fast!

Q: Where's the vector database?
A: rag_service/data/chroma_db/ - persists across sessions

Q: Can I use different embedders?
A: Yes! Change to "openai" or "local" in RAGAgent initialization

Q: What if I need to reindex?
A: Delete chroma_db folder or call rag.clear()

Q: Can I test without Google Drive?
A: Not yet - future support for local files planned

Q: What's the token savings?
A: ~80% fewer tokens (full PDF → 5 relevant chunks)
